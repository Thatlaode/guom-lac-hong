FANTASY SFX - FREE SAMPLER
71 game sound effects for fantasy and RPG games.

Folders:
  wav/  - 44.1 kHz, 16-bit stereo WAV
  ogg/  - same sounds in OGG Vorbis (smaller, ready for Godot / Unity / GameMaker)

Categories: UI, coins, combat, magic, items and doors, footsteps, creatures, explosions,
and seamless ambience loops (forest, lava cave, waterfall).
Each sound comes in up to 3 variations (_01, _02, _03) so repeated actions don't sound identical.
Every file is trimmed, faded and loudness-matched. The *_loop files are crossfaded to loop seamlessly.

AI DISCLOSURE: these sounds were generated with an AI audio model (Stable Audio 3 Small SFX)
and then edited, trimmed, normalized and looped by us.
